# Data sources and licences

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the TunCO2 Pro source code only. The data
files in this folder are compiled from published third-party sources and remain
subject to the terms of those sources. Anyone who redistributes these files, or
values taken from them, is responsible for complying with those terms.

| File | Content | Source | Licence or terms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `factors.csv` (set `current`, grid electricity) | Location-based grid factors, scope 2 + scope 3 | National Greenhouse Accounts Factors 2026, Australian Government Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEEW) | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) |
| `factors.csv` (set `current`, freight transport) | Freight factors per tonne.km | UK Government greenhouse gas conversion factors for company reporting 2026, Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ) | Open Government Licence v3.0 |
| `factors.csv` (set `current`, concrete and steel); `nefd_concrete.csv` | Material emission factors by strength band and product | NABERS National material emission factors database v2026.2 (July 2026), NSW Government | No licence is stated by the publisher; confirmation of redistribution terms has been requested. Reproduced with attribution for research and teaching. |
| `factors.csv` (set `v1`) | Factors of the original toolbox | TunCO2 v1 (Chen et al., 2026, TUST 167, 107028) | As published with TunCO2 v1 |
| `concrete_ecf_database.csv` | Concrete carbon factors compiled for the TunCO2 v1 regression | EPiC, AusLCI, ICE, Oekobaudat and product EPDs, as compiled in Chen et al. (2024), TUST 148, 105776 | Each value remains subject to the terms of its source database |
| `ground_units.csv` | Indicative generic ground parameters | This project | MIT |
| `benchmark_part3_rail.json` | Part 3 baseline used for validation | Chen et al. (2026), TUST 167, 107028 | MIT (values as published) |
| `examples/` | Synthetic reference tunnels | Generated by `tools/make_example_corridors.py` | MIT; no real project data |
